## Blender Keyboard Shortcuts

Shift-A: Most important

Ctrl-A: Apply rotation and scale. Resets scale to 1 for further operations.

TAB: switch between Object Mode and Edit Mode

G: Grab (Move)
 G, then X, move in X direction
 G, then Y, move in Y direction
 G, then Z, move in Z direction, 
R: Rotate
 R, then X, move in X direction
 R, then Y, move in Y direction
 R, then Z, move in Z direction, 
S: Scale
 S, then X, move in X direction
 S, then Y, move in Y direction
 S, then Z, move in Z direction, 

There is something about proportional or uniform scaling, chase that up.
