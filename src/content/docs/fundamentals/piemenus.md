In Blender, these circular pop-up menus are called Pie Menus. They are designed for speed because you do not need to click; you just press the hotkey, flick your trackpad cursor in the direction of the option, and release the key. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
Here is the complete cheat sheet for the most important Pie Menus in Blender, categorized by function.
------------------------------
## 🌐 Navigation & Views (Essential for Trackpads)

* ~ (Tilde / Grave Accent): View Menu. Quickly switch between Front, Top, Right, and Camera views without a numpad.
* , (Comma): Orientation Menu. Changes the transformation axes (Global, Local, Normal) for moving objects.
* . (Period): Pivot Point Menu. Changes the center point for rotations and scaling (e.g., Bounding Box, 3D Cursor, Individual Origins). [6, 7, 8, 9, 10] 

## 🛠️ Modeling & Selection

* Shift + S: Snap Menu. Snaps your selection to the 3D Cursor, or moves the 3D Cursor to your selection. Crucial for precise layout.
* Ctrl + Tab (in Edit Mode): Mesh Selection Mode. Quickly switch between selecting Vertices, Edges, or Faces.
* O (or Shift + O): Proportional Editing Falloff. Changes the shape of the brush influence when pulling geometry (Smooth, Sharp, Random, etc.). [11, 12, 13, 14, 15] 

## 👁️ Display & Shading

* Z: Shading Menu. Instantly toggles between Wireframe, Solid, Material Preview, and Rendered views.
* Alt + Z: X-Ray Mode Toggle. (Standard menu, but can be configured as a pie menu to see through your meshes). [16, 17, 18, 19] 

## 🎬 Animation & Timelines

* Shift + Spacebar: Tools Menu. Pops up your toolbar right at your cursor location so you don't have to drag your trackpad to the left side of the screen.
* Ctrl + Tab (in Object Mode): Mode Switching. Quickly jump between Object Mode, Edit Mode, Sculpt Mode, and Weight Paint. [20, 21, 22, 23] 

------------------------------
## 💡 Trackpad Pro-Tip: "Flick Select"
To use these menus instantly without clicking:

   1. Press and hold the shortcut key (like Z).
   2. Flick your finger on the trackpad toward your choice.
   3. Release the key while your cursor is hovering over the option. [24, 25, 26]
